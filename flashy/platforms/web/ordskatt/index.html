Ordskatt

A word treasure. En ordskatt.

For iPhone and iPad

One Swedish word at a time. Swipe right if you know it, left if you don't. Words you miss come back on a widening schedule — ten minutes, a day, three days, a week — until they stick. Words you know count towards the treasure and never come back.

The hoard: the 36,000 most common Swedish words, ranked by how the language is actually used, with Swedish definitions, English glosses, every inflection, and en or ett where it matters.

What's inside

  • Real frequency ranking — learn the words Swedes actually use, in the order they're worth learning.
  • Profiles for the whole household on one iCloud account: the iPad can be one person's words, the phone another's.
  • Hears and speaks: every word and example read aloud in Swedish.
  • On-device AI explanations on newer iPhones — meaning, usage, and fresh example sentences, written on your phone, nothing sent anywhere.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress lives in your own private iCloud.
