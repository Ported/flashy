Privacy

Ordskatt for iOS · last updated 6 September 2026

The short version

Ordskatt collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server run by the developer.

Your progress

What you do in the app — which words you know, which you're practising, your profiles — is stored on your device and synced through your own private iCloud database, run by Apple and tied to your Apple ID. The developer cannot see, read, or access any of it. Deleting the app and its iCloud data removes it entirely.

On-device features

Pronunciation uses the speech synthesis built into iOS. AI explanations, on devices that support Apple Intelligence, are generated by Apple's on-device model — the words you look up never leave your phone. The optional dictionary lookup uses the dictionaries built into iOS.

Third parties

None. The app makes no network requests to anyone other than Apple's iCloud sync, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
